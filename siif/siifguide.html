InsightED School Head · SIIF Hub

SIIF Hub User Guide

A practical guide for School Heads on submitting the School Innovation and Improvement Fund (SIIF) plan and recording quarterly SIIF utilization. The SIIF plan submission is independent of utilization.

Quick Rule · SIIF Submission: Complete the 5 plan cards in order ➔ Confirm & Certify each card ➔ Review the summary ➔ Type CONFIRM & Submit.
Quick Rule · Utilization: Pick the quarter ➔ Add spending entries ➔ Tick the activities funded ➔ Save.
A

Navigating InsightED

Access the official InsightED portal gateway and open the SIIF Hub module.

Video · Gateway Access

Step 1: Access the SIIF Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/
  2. Click the ENTER INSIGHTED button.
  3. Select HROD Portals from the available portal cards.
  4. Click Continue with the School Head.
  5. Sign in (see Section B), then click the SIIF card on the Nexus dashboard.
B

Login

Create your School Head account, sign in with your password or 6-digit passcode, and recover access if you forget your password.

Video · Create Account & Sign In

Step 1: Sign in with School Credentials

  1. On the School Head Portal, enter your 6-digit School ID.
  2. Enter your Password. To use your quick sign-in code instead, click Switch to 6-Digit Passcode Login and enter your passcode.
  3. Click Sign In.
  4. If prompted with Secure Your Account, set up your 6-digit passcode for faster sign-in next time.

Step 2: Create a New Account (First-Time Users)

  1. On the login screen, click Create New Account.
  2. Primary Details: enter your First Name and Last Name.
  3. Credentials: enter your Official School Email and your Mobile Number (11 digits starting with 09).
  4. School Selection: under Locate Your School, select your region and school. Take note of Your Unique Login Username, which you will always use to sign in.
  5. Set and confirm your Account Password (at least 6 characters), then click Register Account.

Step 3: Forgot Password

  1. Click Forgot Password? on the login screen.
  2. Enter your 6-digit School ID or registered email address, then click Send Verification Code.
  3. Check your email, enter the verification code, and type your new password twice (at least 6 characters).
  4. Click Update Password. Use Resend Code if the email does not arrive.
Still unable to sign in? Coordinate with your Division SBM Coordinator, who can retrieve your active passcode from their account. You may also contact support.stride@deped.gov.ph.

Overview

Understand the SIIF Hub navigation and the two independent tracks: required quarterly Utilization and the SIIF plan submission.

Track What you do
Utilization Every quarter: choose interventions ➔ add spending entries ➔ tick activities funded ➔ Save.

D

SIIF Submission · Dashboard

The landing page when you open SIIF. Review your allocation, plan status, and planned interventions at a glance.

Video · Plan Dashboard

Step 1: Review the School Allocation Overview

  • Total Allocated Budget: The SIIF budget granted to your school.
  • Total Estimated Budget: The total cost of the interventions in your plan.
  • Remaining Funds: Allocation minus Total Estimated Budget.
  • Target Learners: Total learners covered by your planned interventions.
  • Forms Completion: The percentage in the header shows how much of the plan is done, with the current plan status (for example, Plan draft, Plan submitted, or No plan yet).

Step 2: Action Queue & Quick Actions

  1. The Action Queue lists your planned interventions with their estimated budget and learners. Click Details → to see the full implementation details.
  2. Plan Intervention: Opens the Plan Form tab to start or edit your plan.
  3. Export Report: Downloads or prints the school allocation and plan summary.

Step 3: Plan Returned for Revision

  1. If the Division or Regional Office returns your plan, an Action Required · Plan Returned for Revision modal appears showing the Remarks from SDO / RO.
  2. Read the remarks carefully. The acknowledge button unlocks after a short countdown.
  3. Open the Plan Form tab, make the requested corrections, and submit the plan again (see Section VI).

SIIF Submission · Plan Form

The planning workspace where you complete and confirm the 5 plan cards in order.

Video · Plan Form

Step 1: Read the Header Badges

Badge Meaning
Draft Your plan is in progress and not yet submitted.
Submitted Sent to the Division Office and awaiting review.
Reviewed Checked and accepted by the SDO. The plan becomes read-only.
For Revision Returned by the Division Office for changes. Read the remarks, update the plan, and submit again.
Editable / Read-Only Shows whether you can currently change the plan.
Forms Completion % Progress across the 5 cards. Submission requires 100%.

Step 2: Understanding Sequential Locking

1. Priority Improvement Areas ➔ 2. Interventions ➔ 3. Beneficiaries ➔ 4. Activities ➔ 5. Budget Estimation

Each card prepares data for the next. Click Confirm & Certify inside a card to unlock the next one. Each card shows one of these labels:

  • Done The card is confirmed and complete.
  • Needs Input The card is open but has missing data.
  • Locked Complete the previous card first.
I

Priority Improvement Areas

Plan Form Step 1: Identify the priority improvement areas of your school.

Video · Priority Improvement Areas

Step 1: Select Priority Improvement Areas

  1. Open the Priority Improvement Areas card in the Plan Form.
  2. Choose a category, either Access and Quality or Governance.
  3. Select Intermediate Outcome: tap an outcome (for example, IO5: Learners attain learning standards) to expand its priority improvement areas.
  4. Check the priority improvement areas you want to add. Areas already in your list are marked Already added to list.
  5. Select or Review Improvement Areas: reorder your list by dragging (or using the arrow keys), or remove an area you no longer need.

Step 2: Confirm & Certify

  1. Review the Priority Improvement Areas Summary. Make sure the areas align with your school's SIP/AIP.
  2. In the Data Authenticity & Accuracy Declaration, type CONFIRM.
  3. Click Confirm & Certify Priority Areas to unlock the Interventions card.
II

Interventions

Plan Form Step 2: Select the interventions your school will implement.

Video · Interventions

Step 1: Select Interventions

Answer "What type of interventions does your school plan to implement?" by selecting one or more of the 12 interventions:

Intervention Purpose
Remediation Help learners who are significantly behind by addressing gaps in foundational literacy and numeracy.
Enhancement Strengthen regular classroom instruction so learners achieve grade-level competencies.
Enrichment Deepen learning and extend higher-order thinking skills beyond minimum standards.
Prevention Identify and address learning difficulties early so learners do not fall behind.
Wellness Improve readiness and performance by addressing health, nutrition, and physical well-being.
Learning Environment Provide a safe and learner-friendly environment for teaching and learning.
Acceleration Help learners rapidly attain foundational skills by focusing on the most essential competencies.
Inclusive Ensure equitable access to learning, especially for marginalized, at-risk, or special-needs learners.
Engagement Improve participation and retention by reducing absenteeism and dropout and strengthening parent and community support.
Behavioral Remove social, emotional, and behavioral barriers to learning.
Instructional Improvement Enhance teacher effectiveness in literacy and numeracy instruction through professional development.
Technology-enabled Use appropriate technologies to personalize learning and support data-informed teaching.

Step 2: Select Subject Areas for Remediation

  1. If you selected Remediation, choose at least one of its Subject Areas: Reading, Mathematics, Science, or Others.

Step 3: Confirm & Certify

  1. Review the Selected School Interventions and make sure they support your Priority Improvement Areas.
  2. In the Data Authenticity & Accuracy Declaration, type CONFIRM.
  3. Click Confirm & Certify Interventions to unlock the Beneficiaries card.
III

Beneficiaries

Plan Form Step 3: Enter the target learners for each intervention by grade level.

Video · Beneficiaries

Step 1: Select Grade Levels

  1. Answer "Who will be the beneficiaries of each intervention?" by clicking Configure on each intervention.
  2. Select the grade levels covered. Grade levels are grouped by key stage:
Key Stage Grade Levels
Key Stage 1 Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3
Key Stage 2 Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6
Key Stage 3 Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10
Key Stage 4 Grade 11, Grade 12

Step 2: Enter Learner Counts

  1. Enter the number of target learners for each selected grade level. The card shows the Total Learners per intervention.
  2. For Remediation, enter the learner count per Subject Area.
  3. Click Edit on an intervention to change its grade levels or counts.
A grade level with 0 learners is not accepted. If a grade level has no beneficiaries, unselect it. Do not enter your total school population unless the intervention truly covers every learner.

Step 3: Confirm & Certify

  1. Review the Target Beneficiaries Summary and the Total Learners Across Plan.
  2. In the Data Authenticity & Accuracy Declaration, type CONFIRM.
  3. Click Confirm & Certify Beneficiaries to unlock the Activities card.
IV

Activities

Plan Form Step 4: Plan the activities for each intervention.

Video · Activities

Step 1: Select Activities

Answer "What activities does your school plan to conduct?" by clicking Configure on each intervention. Activities are grouped into three categories:

Category Activities
SIP–AIP–Aligned Activities Development or enhancement of learning materials · Conduct of school-level Learning Action Cell (LAC) sessions · Purchase of instructional supplies and materials for classroom use · Purchase of food and ingredients for school-based feeding programs · Minor repairs and improvements to enhance the learning environment · Printing or reproduction of learning materials · Others (specify)
Action Research (AR) Implementation of Action Research or innovative interventions designed to improve learner performance
Remaining SIIF Balance Procurement of semi-expendable items · Conduct of workshops and capacity-building activities · Other day-to-day operational needs
If you choose Others (specify), type a clear description of the activity.

Step 2: Confirm & Certify

  1. Make sure every intervention has at least one activity.
  2. Review the Planned Activities Summary.
  3. In the Data Authenticity & Accuracy Declaration, type CONFIRM.
  4. Click Confirm & Certify Activities to unlock the Budget Estimation card.
V

Budget Estimation

Plan Form Step 5: Enter the estimated budget for each intervention within your allocation.

Video · Budget Estimation

Step 1: Enter Estimated Budgets

  1. Answer "How much is the budget for each intervention?" by entering the Estimated Budget for every intervention. No intervention may be left blank.
  2. Use View Target Beneficiaries Reference to check the learner counts. The card also shows the allocation per individual (estimated budget ÷ number of beneficiaries).

Step 2: Check Against Your Allocation

  1. Compare the Total Estimate with your Allocated amount and the Remaining Balance.
  2. If the total is higher than your allocation (Excess Budget), reduce the estimates before you continue.
Budget Note: Your total must not exceed your official allocation. The card will not let you proceed until the total is within the allocation.

Step 3: Confirm & Certify

  1. Review the Budget Estimations Summary and Total Plan Budget.
  2. In the Data Authenticity & Accuracy Declaration, type CONFIRM.
  3. Click Confirm & Certify Estimates.
If your total is lower than your allocation, a You have unallocated funds prompt shows the Remaining Unallocated Budget. Adjust your estimates, or wait for the countdown and click Proceed Anyway.
VI

Summary & Submission

Plan Form Step 6: Review your Implementation Plan Summary, submit it, and update it later if needed.

Video · Summary & Submission

Step 1: Review the Implementation Plan Summary

  1. When all 5 cards show Done (Forms Completion 100%), click Submit Plan at the bottom of the page.
  2. In the Implementation Plan Summary, check the Interventions, Total Learners, Total Budget, and the Budget vs. Allocation Limit bar.
  3. Review the Target Beneficiaries (Key Stages) and Planned Activities.
If you see PLAN INCOMPLETE, go back and confirm every card. Submission is also blocked if the Total Budget exceeds your allocation.

Step 2: Type CONFIRM & Submit

  1. Type CONFIRM in the confirmation box.
  2. Click Submit Final Plan.
  3. Check that the header badge changes to Submitted.
After submission, wait for the Division Office review. You will see Official Plan Reviewed once it is accepted.

Step 3: Update a Submitted Plan

  1. A submitted plan opens as read-only. Click Edit Plan to make changes.
  2. Edit the cards you need, then Confirm & Certify them again.
  3. Click Submit Plan. The Changes Detected in Submitted Plan modal lists every modification.
  4. Choose Confirm Change to upload your changes as the new submitted version, or Revert Change to discard them and restore the original submitted data.
If nothing was changed, the button reads Submitted (No Changes) and is disabled. Plans already marked Reviewed can no longer be edited.

Record Utilization · Interventions

Record Utilization tab: Turn on tracking for the interventions where your school actually spent its SIIF funds.

Video · Record Utilization · Interventions

Step 1: Select Interventions

  1. Open Utilization ➔ Record Utilization.
  2. In the Interventions panel, click Manage if the list is collapsed.
  3. Tap an intervention to turn tracking on or off (for example, Remediation, Enrichment, Learning Environment, Technology-enabled), then click Done.
  4. Each tracked intervention appears as a card under Recording for the selected quarter.
Select the interventions where your school actually spent its SIIF funds.

Step 2: Stop Tracking an Intervention

  1. On the intervention card, click the remove button (Stop tracking this intervention).
  2. If it already has recorded amounts, confirm the Remove [intervention]? prompt.
Removing an intervention deletes its amounts from your saved records right away. An intervention with entries in a locked quarter cannot be removed.
II

Record Utilization · Spending & Activities

Record Utilization tab: Add spending, choose the activities funded, and save for each quarter.

Video · Record Utilization · Spending & Activities

Step 1: Select the Quarter

  1. Use the quarter tabs at the top: July - September, October - December, and January - March. A green dot marks the Current quarter.
  2. Each tab shows the quarter's total and how many interventions are recorded (e.g. 2/3 recorded).
  3. A Locked quarter can be viewed but not changed. It opens when the division enables it.

Step 2: Add Spending

  1. On an intervention card, click + Add spending.
  2. Enter the Amount spent, then click Add entry.
  3. Repeat for each expense. Utilized this quarter on the card is the total of its spending entries.
  4. Click the list icon (View expense breakdown) to open the Expense breakdown. Click an entry's pencil to change it (Update entry) or remove it (Delete this entry).
Each card shows School balance left. An entry cannot be higher than the balance left, and you cannot add spending to a quarter that has not started yet.

Step 3: Choose the Activities Funded

  1. Tap the Activities funded row on the card to open the checklist.
  2. Check every activity the amount was spent on, grouped under SIP / AIP, Action Research, and Operational. Activities from your SIIF plan are tagged Planned.
  3. If you choose Others (specify), type the activity in the text box.
  4. Click Done.
  5. Optionally, add notes or an accomplishment description in Remarks / Justification.
Every quarter with an amount needs at least one activity. Otherwise, saving is blocked with an Activities needed message.

Step 4: Save Changes

  1. Click Save changes on the card, or on the floating Unsaved changes bar, which lists every card with changes.
  2. Wait for the Utilization updates saved confirmation. The bar then shows All changes saved.
Changes stay on this page until you click Save. Once saved, your school and division records are updated and the Dashboard shows the new figures.

Troubleshooting & Checklist

Quick solutions to common messages in Utilization and the SIIF plan.

Step 1: Resolve Common Utilization Issues

Message / Issue Likely cause Action
Activities needed A quarter has an amount but no activity checked. Open Activities funded on the highlighted card and check at least one.
Over allocation limit / Over budget Total spending would exceed your school allocation. Lower or delete entries so the total stays within the allocation.
"[Quarter] is locked" (e.g. Jul – Sep is locked) The division has not opened, or has closed, that quarter. View only. Wait for the quarter to open.
Utilization entry is currently closed All quarters are locked. You can still view records; contact your Division Office if you need to record.
"… hasn't started yet" The selected quarter is in the future. Record spending once the quarter begins.
"… can't be removed" The intervention has entries in a locked quarter. Those records must stay; keep the intervention.

Step 2: Resolve Common Plan Issues

Issue Likely cause Action
A card is Locked The previous card is not confirmed. Open the earlier card and click Confirm & Certify.
Submit Plan is disabled Forms Completion is below 100%, or the submitted plan has no changes. Finish every card, or click Edit Plan and make your changes.
Submit Final Plan is disabled Not all cards are confirmed, or the Total Budget exceeds the allocation. Confirm & Certify every card and keep the budget within the allocation.
Confirmation box turns red CONFIRM was not typed exactly. Type CONFIRM, then click Submit Final Plan again.
SIIF card returns to Nexus The module is temporarily closed. Try again later.
