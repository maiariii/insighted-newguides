InsightED School Head · SIIF Hub

SIIF Hub User Guide

A practical guide for School Heads on recording quarterly SIIF utilization and, when needed, submitting the optional School Innovation and Improvement Fund plan.

Quick Rule: Record spending in Utilization every quarter ➔ Add spending entries and tick the activities funded ➔ Save. The SIIF Submission plan is optional, has no deadline, and does not affect Utilization.
A

Navigating InsightED

Access the official InsightED portal gateway and open the SIIF Hub module.

Video · Gateway Access

Step 1: Access the SIIF Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/
  2. Click the ENTER INSIGHTED button.
  3. Select HROD Portals from the available portal cards.
  4. Click Continue with the School Head.
  5. Sign in (see Section B), then click the SIIF card on the Nexus dashboard.
Module availability: SIIF can be temporarily closed by the system administrators (for example, during maintenance). When it is closed, clicking the SIIF card returns you to the Nexus dashboard. Try again later or check announcements from your Division Office.
B

Login

Sign into the School Head Portal with your password or 6-digit passcode, and recover access if you forget your password.

Video · Account Sign In

Step 1: Sign in with School Credentials

  1. On the School Head Portal, enter your 6-digit School ID.
  2. Enter your Password. To use your quick sign-in code instead, click Switch to 6-Digit Passcode Login and enter your passcode.
  3. Click Sign In.
  4. If prompted with Secure Your Account, set up your 6-digit passcode for faster sign-in next time.

Step 2: Forgot Password

  1. Click Forgot Password? on the login screen.
  2. Enter your 6-digit School ID or registered email address, then click Send Verification Code.
  3. Check your email, enter the verification code, and type your new password twice (at least 6 characters).
  4. Click Update Password. Use Resend Code if the email does not arrive.
Still unable to sign in? Use Retrieve Passcode or contact helpdesk.stride@deped.gov.ph. Your Division Office can also look up your active passcode.

Step 3: Verify School Identity & Fiscal Year

  1. Check that your School Name appears in the page header and the sidebar, and that the Fiscal Year (FY) shown is correct.
Security Warning: If the wrong school appears, log out immediately. Never enter or modify data under another school's ID.
C

Overview

Understand the SIIF Hub navigation and the two independent tracks: required quarterly Utilization and the optional SIIF plan.

Step 1: Navigation Menu Breakdown

On desktop the menu is a sidebar on the left. On phones and tablets it is a bar at the bottom of the screen.

Menu Item Primary Purpose
SIIF Submission Optional View your SIIF plan dashboard and prepare, submit, or update the optional SIIF plan. Has two tabs: Dashboard and Plan Form.
Nexus Center button that returns you to the InsightED Nexus dashboard.
Utilization Record quarterly spending per intervention. Has three tabs: Dashboard, Record Utilization, and History.
Settings View your profile and manage your password and passcode.
Sign Out Securely log out of InsightED.
Unsaved changes: If you try to leave a page with unsaved edits (switching menus, going to Nexus, or signing out), the system asks you to confirm first so your work is not lost.

Step 2: The Two SIIF Tracks

Track Required? What you do
Utilization Required Every quarter: choose interventions ➔ add spending entries ➔ tick activities funded ➔ Save.
SIIF Submission (Plan) Optional · No Deadline Complete the 5 plan cards ➔ review summary ➔ type CONFIRM ➔ submit for Division review.
The two tracks are independent. You can record Utilization even if you never submit a SIIF plan.
D

SIIF Submission · Dashboard

The landing page when you open SIIF. Review your allocation, plan status, and planned interventions at a glance.

Video · Plan Dashboard
Updated video coming soon A new walkthrough of this screen is being recorded.

Step 1: Review the School Allocation Overview

  • Total Allocated Budget: The SIIF budget granted to your school.
  • Total Estimated Budget: The total cost of the interventions in your plan.
  • Remaining Funds: Allocation minus Total Estimated Budget.
  • Target Learners: Total learners covered by your planned interventions.
  • Forms Completion: The percentage in the header shows how much of the plan is done, with the current plan status (for example, Plan draft, Plan submitted, or No plan yet).

Step 2: Action Queue & Quick Actions

  1. The Action Queue lists your planned interventions with their estimated budget and learners. Click Details → to see the full implementation details.
  2. Plan Intervention: Opens the Plan Form tab to start or edit your plan.
  3. Export Report: Downloads or prints the school allocation and plan summary.

Step 3: Plan Returned for Revision

  1. If the Division or Regional Office returns your plan, an Action Required · Plan Returned for Revision window appears showing the Remarks from SDO / RO.
  2. Read the remarks carefully. The acknowledge button unlocks after a short countdown.
  3. Open the Plan Form tab, make the requested corrections, and submit the plan again (see Section VI).
E

SIIF Submission · Plan Form

The optional planning workspace where you complete and confirm the 5 plan cards in order.

Optional · No Deadline: Submitting a SIIF plan is optional. Use it if your plan was lost during the September 2026 data incident or you have not submitted yet. It does not affect your Utilization.

Step 1: Read the Header Badges

Badge Meaning
Draft Your plan is in progress and not yet submitted.
Submitted Sent to the Division Office and awaiting review.
Reviewed Checked and accepted by the SDO. The plan becomes read-only.
Rejected Disapproved by the Division Office. Read the remarks banner and correct the plan.
Editable / Read-Only Shows whether you can currently change the plan.
Forms Completion % Progress across the 5 cards. Submission requires 100%.

Step 2: Understanding Sequential Locking

1. Priority Improvement Areas ➔ 2. Interventions ➔ 3. Beneficiaries ➔ 4. Activities ➔ 5. Budget Estimation

Each card prepares data for the next. Click Confirm & Certify inside a card to unlock the next one. Each card shows one of these labels:

  • Done The card is confirmed and complete.
  • Needs Input The card is open but has missing data.
  • Locked Complete the previous card first.

Step 3: Availability & Lock Messages

Message Meaning / Explanation
Submission Window Scheduled / Opening Soon The Plan Form opens on the scheduled date shown. Click Return to Dashboard in the meantime.
Window Closed The submission period has ended. The plan is read-only.
Read-Only The plan can be viewed but not changed (for example, it is already reviewed, or it was submitted and you have not clicked Edit Plan).
Plan Disapproved by Division Office Shows the Division's Remarks / Correction Instructions. Update the plan and submit again.
Connection Error Your plan could not be loaded. Saving is disabled to protect your existing data. Click Retry Loading.

Selecting Priority Improvement Areas

Plan Form Step 1: Identify and confirm the school's primary SIP/AIP priorities.

Step 1: Choose Priority Improvement Areas

  1. Open Priority Improvement Areas in the Plan Form.
  2. Select or enter the school’s most important improvement needs.
  3. Review the selected Priority Improvement Areas to make sure they align with your school's SIP/AIP development plan.

Selecting Interventions

Plan Form Step 2: Select the specific interventions your school will execute.

Video · Select Interventions

Step 1: Choose Intervention Categories

Available intervention area Purpose
Remediation, Enhancement, Enrichment Improve learning recovery, strengthen regular instruction, or extend learning.
Prevention, Wellness, Learning Environment Address early learning risks, health/readiness needs, or classroom conditions.
Acceleration, Inclusive, Engagement Support fast progress, equitable access, participation, and retention.

  1. If choosing Remediation (ARAL), answer the ARAL tutor and subject questions.
  2. Review the selected Interventions and ensure they align with the chosen Priority Improvement Areas.

Inputting Target Beneficiaries

Plan Form Step 3: Enter target learner counts by grade level for each intervention.

Video · Beneficiary Counts

Step 1: Select Grade Levels

Key Stage Grades Covered
Key Stage 1 Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3
Key Stage 2 Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6
Key Stage 3 Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10

Step 2: Enter Learner Counts

  1. Enter the number of target learners per grade level.

IV

Selecting Activities

Plan Form Step 4: Pick the exact activities and materials needed per intervention.

Video · Activity Selection

Step 1: Select Activity Types

Activity group Examples
School improvement and annual planning activities Learning materials, learning sessions, instructional supplies, feeding support, minor repairs, printing or reproduction.
Action research or innovation Research-based or innovative activities designed to improve learner performance.
Operational support activities Semi-expendable items, workshops, capacity-building, and other day-to-day school needs.
Other activity A clearly written activity that does not fit the listed choices.

Step 2: Confirm Activities

  1. Ensure every intervention has at least one active activity selected (if choosing Others, write a clear description).
  2. Review all selected activities to ensure they are appropriate and feasible for each intervention.
  3. Click Confirm & Certify Activities to unlock Budget Estimation.
V

Inputting of Budget Estimation

Plan Form Step 5: Encode cost estimates and ensure they stay within your allocation ceiling.

Video · Budget Entry

Step 1: Enter Realistic Costs

  1. Input the estimated budget for each intervention.
  2. No intervention should be left with a zero (0) or blank budget.

Step 2: Check Budget Ceiling

  1. Compare Total Estimated Budget with your Total Allocation.
  2. If estimated budget is higher, reduce costs or activities before submitting.
  3. Review all estimated itemized costs to ensure accuracy before confirming.
  4. Click Confirm & Certify Estimates.
The system will block submission if your total estimate exceeds your school's allocation.
VI

Summary & Submission

Plan Form Step 6: Review your consolidated plan, submit it, and update it later if needed.

Video · Final Submission

Step 1: Review the Implementation Plan Summary

  1. When all 5 cards show Done (Forms Completion 100%), click Submit Plan at the bottom of the page.
  2. In the Implementation Plan Summary, verify the total Interventions, Total Learners, Total Budget, and the Budget vs. Allocation Limit bar.
  3. Review the target beneficiaries per key stage and the planned activities.
If you see PLAN INCOMPLETE, go back and confirm every card. Submission is also blocked if the total budget exceeds your allocation.

Step 2: Type CONFIRM & Submit

  1. Type CONFIRM in the confirmation box.
  2. Click Submit Final Plan.
  3. Check that the header badge changes to Submitted.
After submission, wait for the Division Office review. You will see Official Plan Reviewed once it is accepted.

Step 3: Update a Submitted Plan

  1. A submitted plan opens as read-only. Click Edit Plan to make changes.
  2. Edit the cards you need and confirm them again.
  3. Click Submit Plan. The Changes Detected in Submitted Plan window lists every modification.
  4. Choose Confirm Change to upload your changes as the new submitted version, or Revert Change to discard them and restore the original submitted data.
If nothing was changed, the button reads Submitted (No Changes) and is disabled. Plans already marked Reviewed can no longer be edited.

I

Choosing Interventions to Track

Record Utilization Step 1: Turn on tracking for the interventions your school is spending SIIF funds on.

Video · Select Interventions
Updated video coming soon A new walkthrough of this screen is being recorded.

Step 1: Select Interventions

  1. Open Utilization ➔ Record Utilization.
  2. In the Interventions panel, tap an intervention to turn tracking on or off (for example, Remediation, Enrichment, Learning Environment, Technology-enabled).
  3. Each selected intervention appears as a card under Recording for the selected quarter.
Interventions do not have to match your SIIF plan. Choose the ones your school actually spent funds on.

Step 2: Stop Tracking an Intervention

  1. Click the remove button on the intervention card (Stop tracking this intervention).
  2. If it already has recorded amounts, confirm the Remove …? prompt.
Removing an intervention deletes its amounts from your saved records right away. An intervention with entries in a locked quarter cannot be removed.
II

Recording Quarterly Spending

Record Utilization Step 2: Add spending entries, tick the activities funded, and save for each quarter.

Video · Record Spending
Updated video coming soon A new walkthrough of this screen is being recorded.

Step 1: Pick the Quarter

  1. Use the quarter tabs (July – September, October – December, January – March) at the top. A green dot marks the Current quarter.
  2. Each tab shows the quarter's total and how many interventions have been recorded (e.g. 2/3 recorded).
  3. A 🔒 Locked quarter can be viewed but not changed. It opens when the division enables it.

Step 2: Add Spending Entries

  1. On an intervention card, click + Add spending.
  2. Enter the Amount spent, then click Add entry.
  3. Repeat for each expense. The card's Utilized this quarter amount is the total of its entries.
  4. Click the list icon to open the Expense breakdown. Use the pencil to edit an entry, or Delete this entry to remove it.
Allocation cap: Each card shows School balance left. An entry cannot be higher than the balance left, and you cannot add spending to a quarter that has not started yet.

Step 3: Tick the Activities Funded

  1. Tap the Activities funded row on the card to open the checklist.
  2. Tick every activity the amount was spent on. Activities from your SIIF plan are marked for easy reference.
  3. If you choose Others, specify the other activity in the text box.
  4. Click Done. Optionally add notes or an accomplishment description in Remarks.
Every quarter with an amount needs at least one activity. Otherwise saving is blocked with an Activities needed message.

Step 4: Save Your Changes

  1. Click Save changes on the card, or use the floating save bar that lists all unsaved changes.
  2. Wait for the Utilization updates saved confirmation.
Entries stay on the page until you click Save. Once saved, your school and division records are updated and the Dashboard reflects the new figures.
III

Utilization History

Record Utilization Step 3: Check what was saved and when.

Step 1: Review the History Tab

  1. Open Utilization ➔ History.
  2. Review each saved event (Saved utilization or Removed an intervention) and the spending entries per intervention and quarter.
  3. Entries marked Recorded before tracking are amounts saved before itemized entries were introduced. Entries marked Not saved yet still need you to click Save.
G

Troubleshooting & Checklist

Quick solutions to common messages in Utilization and the SIIF plan.

Step 1: Resolve Common Utilization Issues

Message / Issue Likely cause Action
Activities needed A quarter has an amount but no activity ticked. Open Activities funded on the highlighted card and tick at least one.
Over allocation limit / Over budget Total spending would exceed your school allocation. Lower or delete entries so the total stays within the allocation.
Quarter is locked The division has not opened, or has closed, that quarter. View only. Wait for the quarter to open.
Utilization entry is currently closed All quarters are locked. You can still view records; contact your Division Office if you need to record.
"… hasn't started yet" The selected quarter is in the future. Record spending once the quarter begins.
"… can't be removed" The intervention has entries in a locked quarter. Those records must stay; keep the intervention.

Step 2: Resolve Common Plan Issues

Issue Likely cause Action
A card is Locked The previous card is not confirmed. Open the earlier card and click Confirm & Certify.
Submit Plan is disabled Forms Completion is below 100%, or the submitted plan has no changes. Finish every card, or click Edit Plan and make your changes.
Submit Final Plan is disabled Total budget exceeds the allocation or CONFIRM was not typed. Reduce the budget and type CONFIRM exactly.
SIIF card returns to Nexus The module is temporarily closed. Try again later.
