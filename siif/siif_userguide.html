SIIF Hub User Guide

A practical guide for School Heads on preparing, reviewing, submitting, and monitoring the School Innovation and Improvement Fund plan.

Operating rule: Complete the SIIF Hub in order: review allocation, prepare the plan, confirm all required sections, submit for review, then update utilization only when the utilization period is available.
A

Understand the SIIF Hub flow

The SIIF Hub helps the school prepare an investment plan, identify learners and activities, estimate costs, submit the plan for review, and later record utilization.

Step 1: Open the SIIF Hub

  1. Sign in using the School Head account.
  2. Open the SIIF Hub from the main InsightED School Head portal.
  3. Confirm that the school name and school year or fiscal year shown on the page are correct.
If the wrong school appears, stop and sign out. Do not enter SIIF data under the wrong school account.

Step 2: Use the SIIF navigation

Navigation itemUse it for
DashboardChecking allocation, estimated budget, remaining funds, status, and quick actions.
FormsPreparing or revising the SIIF plan.
UtilizationRecording quarterly spending and implementation progress after the plan is ready for tracking.
SettingsViewing or managing available SIIF settings when allowed by the system.

The Dashboard is the starting point for checking whether the school has enough allocation for the proposed plan.

Step 1: Review the dashboard figures

  • Total Estimated Budget
  • Remaining Funds
  • Budget Utilized
  • Fiscal year
  • Forms completion percentage
Use the allocation amount as the spending limit when preparing the plan.

Step 2: Check proposal status before editing

Step 3: Use Quick Actions correctly

  1. Plan Intervention opens the form for creating or editing the proposal.
  2. Review Flagged Items helps you return to incomplete sections.
  3. Export Report lets you print or save the allocation summary.
  4. Open intervention details when you need to review beneficiaries, activities, and budget for a specific item.
C

Forms Hub: required planning sections

The Forms Hub is completed in order. Each section prepares the next one, so avoid skipping ahead.

Step 1: Complete Priority Improvement Areas

  1. Open Priority Improvement Areas.
  2. Select or enter the school’s most important improvement needs.
  3. Confirm the section before moving to interventions.

Step 2: Select interventions

  1. Open Interventions.
  2. Select only the interventions the school will actually implement.
  3. If choosing Remediation (ARAL), answer the related ARAL planning questions.
  4. Confirm the section before moving to beneficiaries.
Available intervention areaPurpose
Remediation, Enhancement, EnrichmentImprove learning recovery, strengthen regular instruction, or extend learning.
Prevention, Wellness, Learning EnvironmentAddress early learning risks, health/readiness needs, or classroom conditions.
Acceleration, Inclusive, EngagementSupport fast progress, equitable access, participation, and retention.
Behavioral, Instructional Improvement, Technology-enabledAddress social-emotional barriers, teacher effectiveness, or technology-supported learning.

Step 3: Respect sequential locking

  1. Finish Priority Improvement Areas first.
  2. Finish Interventions before Beneficiaries.
  3. Finish Beneficiaries before Activities.
  4. Finish Activities before Budget.
  5. If you change an earlier section, review the sections after it again.
D

Beneficiaries and learner counts

Beneficiaries identify who will be served by each intervention. Counts should be realistic and should match the target learners for the planned activity.

Step 1: Select the target grade levels

GroupGrade levels
Key Stage 1Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3
Key Stage 2Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6
Key Stage 3Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10
Key Stage 4Grade 11, Grade 12

Step 2: Enter beneficiary counts

  1. For each selected intervention, choose the grade levels that will benefit.
  2. Enter the number of target learners per grade level.
  3. For ARAL remediation, add subject-related learner counts when the form asks for them.
  4. Review totals before confirming.
Avoid entering the whole school population unless the intervention truly serves the whole school.

Step 3: Confirm beneficiary data before moving on

  1. Review every intervention’s target grade levels and learner counts.
  2. Make sure no selected intervention is left with zero beneficiaries.
  3. Confirm Beneficiaries, then proceed to Activities.
E

Activities and implementation details

Activities explain how the school will carry out each intervention. They should be specific enough for reviewers to understand what will happen.

Step 1: Select activity categories

Activity groupExamples
School improvement and annual planning activitiesLearning materials, learning sessions, instructional supplies, feeding support, minor repairs, printing or reproduction.
Action research or innovationResearch-based or innovative activities designed to improve learner performance.
Operational support activitiesSemi-expendable items, workshops, capacity-building, and other day-to-day school needs.
Other activityA clearly written activity that does not fit the listed choices.

Step 2: Add at least one activity per intervention

  1. Open the Activities section after Beneficiaries is confirmed.
  2. For each intervention, select every applicable activity.
  3. If choosing Others (specify), write a clear description.
  4. Confirm the section after every intervention has an activity.

Step 3: Keep activities reviewable

  1. Avoid vague wording such as “support” or “materials” without details.
  2. Make sure the activity matches the selected intervention and beneficiary group.
  3. Use wording that the Division Office can understand without follow-up.
F

Budget estimation and allocation checks

The Budget section assigns the estimated cost for each selected intervention. The total should stay within the official school allocation.

Step 1: Enter budget estimates per intervention

  1. Open Budget Estimation only after Activities is confirmed.
  2. Enter an estimated amount for each selected intervention.
  3. Use the full expected cost of the intervention, not only a partial cost.
  4. Confirm the section after every selected intervention has a reasonable budget.

Step 2: Compare total budget with allocation

  1. Review the total learners, total activities, and total estimated budget.
  2. Compare the estimated budget against the official allocation.
  3. If the estimate is over the allocation, reduce scope or reprioritize before submission.
The plan should not be submitted if the total budget is higher than the official allocation or if the allocation has not loaded.

Step 3: Review budget quality

  1. Check that each budget amount supports the selected activities.
  2. Make sure no intervention has a zero or blank budget.
  3. Use realistic estimates that can be justified during review.
G

Save draft, review summary, and submit

The SIIF Hub may save draft changes while you work, but you should still review the summary carefully before final submission.

Step 1: Let draft saving finish

  1. After changing a section, pause briefly before closing the browser or moving away.
  2. If you see a saving indicator, wait until it finishes.
  3. If an error appears, check your internet connection and try saving again.

Step 2: Use the summary view

  1. Click View Summary after completing all planning sections.
  2. Review the number of interventions, target learners, activities, and total budget.
  3. Review the details for every intervention.
  4. If something is wrong, return to the relevant section before submitting.

Step 3: Submit final plan

  1. Confirm all planning sections.
  2. Type CONFIRM exactly when the system asks for final attestation.
  3. Click Submit Final Plan.
  4. Wait for the success message and check that the Dashboard status changes to Submitted.
After submission, wait for Division Office review before treating the plan as ready for utilization tracking.
H

Deadline window and read-only states

The SIIF Hub may open and close based on the official submission schedule. When the window is closed, some items may become read-only.

Step 1: Check whether the form is open

  1. Look for deadline or opening schedule notices at the top of the SIIF page.
  2. If the form is not yet open, prepare your data offline but wait before submitting.
  3. If the form is closed, review existing data but do not expect edits to save.

Step 2: Interpret common lock messages

  1. Opening Soon / Not Yet Open: the form is scheduled for a later date.
  2. Window Closed: the submission deadline has passed.
  3. Read-Only Mode: the plan can be viewed but cannot be changed.
  4. Pending Review: the plan has been submitted and is awaiting action from the Division Office.

Step 3: Respond to submission issues

MessageWhat it meansWhat to do
Submission window not openThe form is scheduled to open later.Wait for the official opening date.
Submission window closedThe deadline has passed.Contact the appropriate office if changes are still needed.
Missing required informationOne or more required sections are incomplete.Return to Forms and complete the missing details.
Unable to save or submitA connection or system issue may have occurred.Retry, then report the issue if it continues.
I

Utilization tracking after the plan

Utilization tracking records how much of the approved plan has been used and the implementation status of each intervention.

Step 1: Confirm utilization is available

  1. Open Utilization from the SIIF navigation.
  2. If the planning phase is still active, return to the plan and wait for the tracking period.
  3. If no submitted baseline plan exists, utilization cannot proceed.
  4. If the plan is still pending review, wait for the Division Office review.
  5. If the plan is disapproved, address the remarks first if the schedule allows revision.

Step 2: Use the active quarter only

QuarterMonths covered
Phase 1July, August, September
Phase 2October, November, December
Phase 3January, February, March
Only the active reporting period should be updated. Other periods may be locked to prevent accidental entries.

Step 3: Enter amount, status, and justification

  1. For each planned intervention, enter the amount utilized for the active quarter.
  2. Select the implementation status: Not Yet Started, Ongoing, or Completed.
  3. If the amount is higher than the original estimate for that intervention, provide a clear justification.
  4. Click Save Quarterly Updates and wait for confirmation.

K

Pre-submission and post-submission checklist

Use this final checklist before typing CONFIRM or before reporting a plan as ready for review.

Step 1: Check completeness

Step 2: Check review quality

  1. Make sure priority areas, interventions, beneficiaries, activities, and budgets tell one coherent story.
  2. Remove interventions with zero beneficiaries, zero budget, or no activities.
  3. Use remarks or Other activity text only when it adds clear review value.
  4. Print or save the report if the school needs an internal copy before submission.

Step 3: Track what happens after submission

  1. The Dashboard should show the plan as Submitted or Pending Review.
  2. If reviewed, move to utilization when the tracking period opens.
  3. If disapproved, read the Division Office remarks and revise if the deadline window allows editing.
  4. When utilization opens, update only the active quarter and save quarterly updates.
Final operating rule: Move through SIIF in order: allocation review → plan draft → confirmed sections → final submission → review status → utilization tracking.
SIIF Hub flow: verify school and allocation → complete planning sections → type CONFIRM and submit → wait for review → track utilization by active quarter.
