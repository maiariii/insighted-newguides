InsightED eSF7 · RO / SDO Monitoring Portal

Regional & Schools Division Office User Guide for eSF7 Monitoring

An operational guide for Regional and Division monitoring teams to track school submissions, audit personnel workloads, and generate consolidated reports.

Operating rule: Track division submissions, verify personnel rosters and teacher workloads, audit overload claims, and resolve validation errors before certifying consolidated reports.
A

Access & Sign In to the eSF7 Review Module

Step 1: Access the eSF7 Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official portal link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/
  2. Click the ENTER INSIGHTED button.
  3. Select HROD Portals from the available portal cards.
  4. Click Continue as RO/SDO Personnel.
  5. Click the eSF7 Review card to launch the module.

Step 2: Sign in with Official Account Credentials (Login)

  1. Enter your registered official DepEd Email address or School ID.
  2. Enter your authorized Password. If logging in via Passcode, toggle to the passcode option and input your designated quick sign-in code.
  3. Click the Sign In button to log into the platform.
B

Home Tab & Monitoring Workspace Navigation

The Home tab serves as the central operational hub for Regional and Division monitoring teams, allowing users to move from overall division status down to individual school and personnel records.

Step 1: Executive Dashboard & High-Level Compliance Monitoring

  1. Use the available filters to narrow data by Division, Municipality, District, Submission Status, or School Type.
  2. Review key metric cards at the top: Total Schools, Submitted, Not Yet Submitted, and Submission Rate.
  3. Select a district from the District Breakdown table to view the schools under that district.

Step 2: District School Directory Drilldown

  1. Use the search bar to locate a school by name or 6-digit School ID.
  2. Review the school's basic information, personnel count, and submission status.
  3. Click View Profile → on the school card to open the school's detailed information.

Step 3: Submission Distribution by Location

  1. Toggle between Bars, Heatmap, or Sorting to visualize and rank school submissions.
  2. Use the breadcrumb trail to track your current location, or click Reset Drill to return to the main dashboard.

Step 4: School Profile & Capacity Overview

  1. Review the school head details, total enrollment, organized sections, and teacher-to-learner ratio.
  2. Click View Personnel → or select the List of Personnel tab to view individual staff records.

Step 5: List of Personnel & Workload Audit

  1. Use the search bar to find personnel by name, position, or employee number.
  2. Review workload counters for Total Staff, Regular Load, Confirmed, and Pending.
  3. Click ← Back to Profile to return to the school overview, or click Close (✕) to return to the Home Dashboard.
C

Teaching Overload Claims Monitoring

The Teaching Overload Claims module enables monitoring officers to audit declared teaching overload hours, verify compensation estimates based on salary grades, and certify claims for official payroll processing.

Review and Verify Overload Claims

  • Check the teacher’s Name, PRN, Position, Salary Grade, Overload Hours, Estimated Payment, and Reason for Overload.
  • For Pending claims, click Confirm on the record, review the details in the confirmation modal, and click Confirm Verification to mark the claim as Verified.
  • If a verified claim needs to be reviewed again, click Verified on the row, review the details, and click Remove Verification to return it to Pending.
  • Use Print or Download to generate the required reports.
D

Designations Monitoring

The Designations Monitoring module enables monitoring officers to inspect teacher special assignments, verify official committee roles across functional domains, and certify designations for division records.

Review and Confirm Designations

  • Review the personnel roster details: Personnel Name & PRN, Base Plantilla Position, Domain Category, Grade Level or Subject Area, Designation Title, and Status.
  • For Pending assignments, click Confirm in the Actions column, review the details in the confirmation modal, and click Confirm Designation to mark the assignment as Confirmed.
  • If an assignment needs re-evaluation, click Revoke on the confirmed row and confirm the prompt to return the designation to Pending.
  • Click Print or Download to export the official Division Designations Masterlist or save as a compliance-ready PDF.
E

Allowance Monitoring

The Allowance Monitoring module enables monitoring officers to track school allowance claims, review individual personnel entitlements across statutory allowance categories, and verify or update encoded amounts.

Step 1: View School Records

  • Check the School Records table for School ID, School Name, Division, District, Personnel Count, Total Entitled (₱), and Status (Submitted, Pending, or No Claims).
  • Click a school row or View Matrix to inspect its personnel allowance records.
  • Use the pagination controls to browse 10 schools per page.

Step 2: Review the Allowance Matrix

  • Review personnel eligibility and allowance amounts across key categories:
    Uniform Teaching / Supplies Medical Special Hardship
  • Eligible personnel display a checkmark with an editable ₱ input box to adjust amounts, while ineligible allowances remain locked.
  • Total Entitled amounts are calculated automatically by the system.

Step 3: Submit or Edit Allowances

  • For first-time submissions: Enter the allowance amounts, click Submit, review the popup, and click Confirm & Submit.
  • To edit or resubmit: Click the ✏️ Pencil icon, update the amounts, click Resubmit, and confirm the changes.

Step 4: Print & Export

  • Click Print / Save as PDF at the top right to generate the official eSF7 report with DepEd header and signatory lines.
  • Click Download CSV to export records for offline auditing or payroll processing.
InsightED eSF7 Operational Directive: Regional and Division Monitoring Officers must conduct regular quality assurance audits throughout the encoding cycle. All eSF7 data must be certified and reconciled prior to national planning deadlines. For technical support, contact the InsightED Help Desk or your Division ICT Coordinator.
