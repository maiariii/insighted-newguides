How to Log In & Access Your Account

Access your existing InsightED SDO account or register a new account with your division's official authorization code.

Video · Sign In & Registration
Already have an InsightED SDO Account? If you already use InsightED (e.g., for SIIF, user lookup, or esf7), you do not need a new account! Simply use your existing email and password.

Step 1: Logging In with an Existing Account

  1. Type in your official DepEd Email and Password.
  2. Click Sign In.

Step 2: First-Time Registration (If you don't have an account yet)

  1. Click Register on the login screen.
  2. Enter your full name, official DepEd email, and create a password.
  3. Select your assigned Region and Division (e.g., Region V – Division of Camarines Sur).
  4. Select your role as School Division Office.
  5. When asked for the Authorization Code, coordinate with the STRIDE Team and email them at support.stride@deped.gov.ph.
  6. Input the authorization code provided by the STRIDE Team, complete registration, and sign in.
Access Protection: Ensure the Authorization Code is only requested and entered by designated SDO personnel responsible for division classification submissions.

Filling Out the Survey (Step-by-Step)

The survey is broken down into 7 simple steps. Select each step below from the sidebar or click through the wizard tabs.

Step 1: Overview of the 7 Survey Steps

STEP NUMBER STEP TITLE CORE DATA CAPTURED
Step 1 Current Classification Select Small, Medium, Large, or Very Large Division.
Step 2 Total School Count Operational schools with ID, without ID, To date.
Step 3 Authorized Teachers Total Authorized Teachers, Elementary & Secondary balance rule.
Step 4 School Districts Total public School Districts in the division.
Step 5 Authorized PSDS Count Total authorized PSDS positions.
Step 6 Geography & Access Part A: 5 Farthest Schools & Terrain %; Part B: Island schools.
Step 7 Review & Final Submission Validation check, summary cards, and confirmation modal.

II

Step 2: Total School Count

Record the active schools under your division's jurisdiction.

Video · Step 2: Total School Count

Step 1: Enter School Figures

  • Operational Schools with School ID: Enter the number of schools that have an official 6-digit DepEd School ID.
  • Operational Schools without School ID: Enter newly established, annexed, or operating schools that do not have a permanent ID yet. (Enter 0 if you don't have any).
  • Total School Count: You do not need to compute this! The system will automatically add the two numbers together for you.
  • As of Date: Select the cutoff date of your records (defaults to today's date).

Click Next Step.

III

Step 3: Authorized Teachers

Record your division's total plantilla/authorized teacher positions.

Video · Step 3: Authorized Teachers

Step 1: Enter Teacher Plantilla Counts

  • Total Authorized Teachers: The overall total number of authorized teacher items in your division.
  • Elementary Teacher Count: Number of authorized teachers assigned to Elementary schools.
  • Secondary Teacher Count: Number of authorized teachers assigned to Junior and Senior High schools.
The Balance Rule: Elementary Teachers + Secondary Teachers MUST equal the Total Authorized Teachers! A live balance indicator on your screen will show a green checkmark when your numbers match perfectly. If they do not match, the system will not allow you to proceed to the next step.

Click Next Step.

VI

Step 6: Geography, Terrain & Access

This step captures the logistical realities of your division across two parts.

Video · Step 6: Geography, Terrain & Access

Part A: Select 5 Farthest Schools

You must identify exactly 5 of the farthest or most difficult-to-reach schools from your SDO.

  1. Search & Select: Click the search box in the dropdown. Type the name, School ID, or municipality of the school. Click on the school to add it. Repeat until you have added 5 schools.
  2. For each of the 5 schools, fill in:
    • Distance (in km): One-way distance in kilometers from your SDO (e.g., 72.5).
    • Travel Time: Enter estimated travel time in hours and minutes for each mode:
      • By Land (vehicle, motorcycle, etc.)
      • By Water (boat, motorized banca)
      • By Walking / Hiking .
    • Primary Mode of Transport: Select how people usually reach the school (e.g., Habal-habal/Motorcycle, Public Van/Bus, Boat/Banca, Walking).
    • Terrain Breakdown (%): Estimate the percentage breakdown of the travel route:
      • Hilly / Rugged %
      • Flat Land %
      • Mountainous / Water %
      100% Rule: These three terrain percentages must add up to exactly 100% (e.g., 50 + 30 + 20).

Part B: Island Municipalities & Schools (Optional)

  • If your division covers island municipalities, select them from the list.
  • Tag any specific island schools that require sea crossing.
  • If your division is purely landlocked, you can leave this section blank.

E

What Happens After Submitting?

Understand confirmation notifications, protected mode, and making corrections.

Step 1: Submission Confirmation & Synchronization

  • A green notification will confirm: SDO Reclassification Survey response submitted successfully!
  • The survey enters a Protected Submitted Mode to prevent accidental edits.
  • Your submitted data is securely synchronized to the central office databases.

Step 2: Making Corrections (Editing a Submitted Survey)

If you realize you made a typo or your division records are updated:

  1. Re-open the SDO Size Classification Study card.
  2. Click the Edit/Update Response button at the top right.
  3. Make your desired changes on any step.
  4. Go to Step 7 and click Submit Survey Response button..
F

Quick Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick solutions to common validation issues and account questions.

Step 1: Problem & Solution Matrix

PROBLEM SIMPLE SOLUTION
"Elementary and Secondary teachers do not match Total" Check your numbers in Step 3. Elementary + Secondary must equal the exact number you typed in Total Authorized Teachers.
"Select exactly 5 farthest schools" In Step 6, make sure you have added 5 different schools and filled in their travel time and terrain percentages.
"Terrain percentages must equal 100%" In Step 6, check the 3 terrain boxes (Hilly, Flat, Mountainous). Their sum must be exactly 100 (e.g., 50 + 30 + 20).

Step 2: Need Help? Technical Support Escalation

If you need technical assistance or need your division assignment adjusted, please contact the STRIDE Team and email them at support.stride@deped.gov.ph.
