SIIF User Guide for Regional & Division Offices

An operational guide for Regional Office (RO) and Schools Division Office (SDO) personnel on monitoring SIIF plan submissions, reviewing school plans, and tracking quarterly fund utilization across beneficiary schools.

Quick Rule · SIIF Plan: Open the Schools tab ➔ Review submitted plans ➔ Confirm Review or request a revision.
Quick Rule · Utilization: Open the Utilization tab ➔ Check each school's quarterly spending ➔ Follow up on schools with no entries.
A

Navigating InsightED

Access the official InsightED portal gateway and open the SIIF Monitoring Hub module.

Video · INSIGHTED Access

Step 1: Access the SIIF Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official portal link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/
  2. Click the ENTER INSIGHTED button.
  3. Select HROD Portals from the available portal cards.
  4. Click Continue as RO/SDO Personnel and sign in (see Section B).
  5. Click the SIIF Monitoring Hub card to launch the module.
System Under Maintenance: If the SIIF Monitoring Hub is temporarily closed, a maintenance screen appears instead of the dashboard. Return to Nexus and try again later.
B

Login and Registration

Sign into your official RO/SDO account, register a new account, and confirm that your office is authorized for SIIF.

Video · Account Sign In & Registration

Step 1: Sign in with Official Account Credentials (Login)

  1. Enter your registered official DepEd Email address.
  2. Enter your Password, or switch to the 6-digit Passcode dialpad and enter your quick sign-in code.
  3. Click Sign In.
  4. Forgot your password? Click Forgot Password?.

Step 2: Create a New Account (Registration)

  1. Click Create New Account on the login screen.
  2. Primary Details: enter your First Name and Last Name, and choose Your Role (RO Personnel or SDO Personnel).
  3. Credentials: enter your DepEd Email Address and add your Contact Number.
  4. Area Alignment: select your Region and Division, then pick your Office/Bureau and Position from the dropdowns. Under Designation, choose Regional SBM Coordinator or Division SBM Coordinator.
  5. Final Security: enter the Authorization Code (request this code from the SIIF team), set and confirm your Account Password, then click Register Account.

Step 3: Confirm Your SIIF Access

Mandatory Access Restriction: The SIIF Monitoring Hub is open only to RO/SDO personnel whose Office is the Field Technical Assistance Division (FTAD) or the School Governance and Operations Division (SGOD). Other offices see an Access Restricted screen with a Return to Nexus button.
SBM Coordinators: Accounts with the Regional or Division SBM Coordinator designation can open only SIIF Monitoring, Registry Management, and User Lookup. For SIIF, their office must still be FTAD or SGOD.
C

Division Overview

Understand the navigation tabs in the Division Office (SDO) view and the plan status labels used throughout the hub.

Step 1: Navigation Tabs Breakdown

NAVIGATION ITEM PRIMARY PURPOSE
Home Division dashboard with two views: Plan Submissions (plan status overview) and Utilization (allocation, spending, and quarterly charts).
Schools List of schools with their SIIF plan status. Open plans and review them, request revisions, or rescind reviews.
Utilization Track quarterly spending per school (July–Sept, Oct–Dec, Jan–March, Remaining) and open each school's declared interventions.
User Lookup Retrieve a School Head's active passcode by 6-digit School ID (within your jurisdiction only).
Settings View your profile and manage your password, passcode, and preferences.

Step 2: Plan Status Labels

Status Meaning Typical Action
Reviewed Checked and accepted by the SDO. None. Rescind only if the review was made in error.
Submitted Submitted, awaiting SDO review. Open the plan and mark it reviewed or request a revision.
For Revision Returned to the school for changes. Click Resolve Revision once the school has submitted its revised plan.
Draft Started by the school but not yet submitted. Monitor only.
No Plan Yet The school has not started a plan yet. Monitor and follow up with the school.
The Plan Submission Rate counts Submitted + Reviewed plans. Schools without a plan can still record utilization.
I

Division Home Tab

Switch between the Plan Submissions and Utilization views to monitor your division at a glance.

Video · Division Dashboard

Step 1: Choose the Home View

  1. Use the switch at the top of Home: Plan Submissions (default) or Utilization.
  2. Use the District filter to narrow the view. Click Reset to clear it, or Refresh to reload the latest data.

Step 2: Read the Plan Submissions View

  • Headline numbers: Plan Submission Rate (schools with a Submitted or Reviewed plan), Reviewed by SDO, For Revision (returned to schools for changes), and Not Yet Submitted (Draft + No Plan Yet).
  • Plan Status: a ring chart and table of schools by current plan status, with counts and percentages.
  • Plan Status by District: submission rate per district, sorted from highest. Switch between Chart and Table.
  • Interventions in Submitted Plans: how many schools plan each intervention.
  • Click Open school plan list to go to the Schools tab.

Step 3: Read the Utilization View

  • KPI cards: Total SIIF Allocation, Total Fund Utilized, Remaining Balance, and Overall Utilization Rate. Hover a card to see its breakdown by district.
  • Filters: District, Quarter, and Intervention. Click Reset to clear them.
  • Intervention Spending by District: declared spending per district, shown by Amount (₱) or No. of Schools. Click a row to drill down to the schools, then click a school to open its details.
  • Quarterly Disbursement by Intervention: spending per intervention for each quarter. Switch between Chart and Table.
II

Division Schools Tab

Monitor each school's SIIF plan, open submitted plans, and take review actions.

Video · Plan Evaluation & Review Action

Step 1: Filter the School List

  • Click a status card (Total Schools, Reviewed, Submitted, For Revision, Draft, No Plan Yet) to filter the list. Use Clear status filter to reset.
  • Search by school name, School ID, or district, or use the Filter… box under each column.
  • Switch between Table and Cards view.
  • Columns include School ID, School Name, District, Plan Status, Allocation, Estimate, % of Alloc., Progress, and Updated.
  • Click the download icon to save the plan list as CSV.
Click View remarks on the For Revision card to see the revision instructions sent to schools.

Step 2: Open a School's Plan

  1. Click a school row to open its plan details.
  2. Check the Allocation Appropriation Percentage (Budget Estimate vs. Total Allocation) and the Priority Improvement Area.
  3. Expand each intervention to review its allocated budget, learners per grade level, and planned activities. Use the download button for a printable report.
  4. You can also take review actions here: Review, For Revision (opens Request Plan Revision; enter what needs to change, then click Submit Request), Manage Plan Revision, or Rescind Review.

Step 3: Take a Review Action

Action When How
Mark as Reviewed Plan is Submitted Click the review (check) button, add optional remarks, then click Confirm Review.
Request Revision Plan needs changes Click the revision button, enter Revision Remarks, then click Request Revision. The school sees your remarks.
Manage / Resolve Revision Plan is For Revision Click the revision button to open Manage Plan Revision. Use Update Revision Remarks to change your instructions, or Resolve Revision once the school has updated its plan (returns it to Submitted).
Rescind Review Plan is Reviewed Click the rescind button, then Rescind Review to revert the plan to Submitted.
A plan that is For Revision cannot be marked reviewed. Resolve the revision first, then review the updated plan.

Step 4: Resolve and Review a Resubmitted Plan

Video · Resolving & Reviewing Resubmitted Plan
  1. Open Manage Plan Revision for the school and click Resolve Revision.
  2. Confirm the prompt. The plan returns to Submitted.
  3. Click the review button again to evaluate the updated plan and Confirm Review.
Role & schedule rules: Review actions are available only to Division (SDO) accounts. If your division set a review period, the buttons are disabled outside it and a banner shows when reviews open or closed (Review period closed).
III

Division Utilization Tab

Monitor quarterly spending declared by the schools in your division.

Video · Utilization Overview

Step 1: Check the Cover Period Cards

  • The JULY-SEPT, OCT-DEC, and JAN-MARCH cards show the Utilized Amount for each quarter. The REMAINING card shows the Remaining Balance.
  • Click a card to open its Quarter Breakdown: detailed school utilization for that period.

Step 2: Review the School List

  1. Check the summary: Schools in Division, Division Allocation, Division Utilized, and Remaining Balance.
  2. Sort the list by School ID, School Name, District, Allocation, Total Utilized, or Remaining Balance.
  3. Click a school row to expand Declared Interventions by Quarter: each intervention's amount per quarter and the school's Remarks / Justification.
A school with no entries shows This school has not declared any SIIF utilization yet.

Step 3: Print or Export

  • Click Print Division Report for a printable report.
  • Click Download CSV to export the figures.
IV

User Lookup Tab

Help a School Head who cannot sign in by retrieving their active passcode.

Video · User Lookup

Step 1: Search the School Account

  1. Open User Lookup.
  2. Enter the school's 6-digit School ID and click Search Account.
  3. Review the School ID, Region, and Division shown.
  4. Click the eye icon to show the Active Passcode, then share it only with the verified School Head.
You can only view accounts within your jurisdiction. Searching outside it shows an Access Denied message. Treat passcodes as confidential credentials.

Adding New Schools

Register a newly established school through the Registry Management Portal. Once registered, notify the School Head to sign in and use SIIF.

Video · Adding New Schools

Step 1: Open Registry Management

  1. From the RO/SDO Nexus dashboard, click the Registry Management card.
  2. Sign in with your registered DepEd Email and Password (or Passcode) if prompted.

Step 2: Click Add New School

  • Select the Add New School tab. Your Region and Division are locked to your session.

Step 3: Input the Necessary Fields

  1. School Identification: School ID (the system warns if it is already registered), School Name, Curricular Offering, and Mother School ID (or NA).
  2. Location Hierarchy: District, Province, Municipality / City, Legislative District, Barangay, and Street Address / Sitio.
  3. Precise Geographic Coordinates: Latitude and Longitude.
Important: Ensure all fields are correct before submitting. Incomplete or incorrect entries may delay or block school registration.

Step 4: Submit Registration & Notify School Heads

  1. Review the details and click Submit Registration.
  2. Track your entries under My Submissions.
  3. Notify the School Head that they can now sign in and use the SIIF Hub.
F

Converting Schools

Register a converted school using its legacy master record. Once converted, inform the School Head of their new School ID.

Video · Converting Schools

Step 1: Open Register Converted

  • In the Registry Management Portal, select the Register Converted tab.

Step 2: Search the Legacy Master Record

  1. Enter the Legacy School ID (6 Digits) and click Search Master Record.
  2. Review the Found Legacy Master Record details (region, division, location, curricular offering, coordinates).
  3. Click Proceed to Conversion Registration →.

Step 3: Fill Up the Necessary Fields

  • In the Convert School form, enter the Target (New) 6-digit School ID.
  • Verify the details and complete all required fields for the newly converted school.
Important: Double-check all details before submitting. Incorrect information may affect the school's records and SIIF data.

Step 4: Submit Conversion & Inform School Heads

  1. Review all details and click Submit for Registration.
  2. Tell the School Head to sign in with the New School ID and the password or passcode of the original school account.
Note for School Heads: Use your New School ID to sign in. Your previous password or passcode remains valid; no reset is needed.
G

Updating School Information & Status

Correct a school's master record or change its operational status in the Registry Management Portal.

Video · Updating School Information & Status

Step 1: Find the School Record

  1. Select the Update School & Status tab.
  2. Enter the 6-digit School ID or IERN and click Find Record.
  3. Permanent identifiers (School ID, IERN, Region, Division) are shown as read-only.

Step 2: Edit Details & Operational Status

  1. Update the school name, Mother School ID, location fields, street address, or coordinates as needed.
  2. Set the Operational Status: Open (Active), Closed (Archived), Merged (Absorbing other schools), Reopened, or Converted.
  3. For Merged, select the schools (same region and division) that this school will absorb. They change to Merged status and sync to this school's IERN.
  4. Enter the Reason for Update / Clearance Justification.

Step 3: Save

  1. Click Save School Updates & Operational Status.
  2. Check the result under My Submissions.
Status changes affect the school's records across InsightED. Double-check the school and justification before saving.

Troubleshooting & Checklist

Quick solutions to common access and data issues, plus an operations checklist.

Step 1: Resolve Common Access & System Issues

Issue / Message Likely Cause Corrective Action
Access Restricted screen Your office is not FTAD or SGOD. Update your office assignment in your profile or contact InsightED support.
System Under Maintenance The SIIF module is temporarily closed. Return to Nexus and try again later.
Review buttons disabled (Review period closed) Outside the division's review period, or you are using an RO account. Check the review banner dates on the Schools tab; reviews are done by SDO accounts.
Cannot mark a plan reviewed The plan is For Revision, or not yet Submitted. Resolve the revision first, or wait for the school to submit.
Could not load plan submissions Network issue or server delay. Click Try again or Refresh.
A school shows no utilization The school has not declared spending yet. Remind the School Head to record quarterly utilization.
User Lookup: Access Denied / not found The school is outside your jurisdiction, or the ID is wrong. Verify the 6-digit School ID and your assigned scope.

Step 3: Plan Evaluation Quality Standards

  • Logical Coherence: Priority areas, interventions, beneficiaries, activities, and budget form one logical plan.
  • No Empty / Zero Items: Request a revision for interventions with zero beneficiaries, no activities, or blank budgets.
  • ARAL Compliance: Verify subject-specific learner counts for remediation.
  • Ceiling Compliance: The plan estimate must not exceed the school allocation (check % of Alloc.).

Step 4: Technical Support Escalation

  1. Document the exact message, School ID, Division, and the step where the issue occurred.
  2. Take a screenshot showing the page header.
  3. Contact the InsightED system support focal person.
