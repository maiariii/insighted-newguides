SIIF User Guide for Regional & Division Offices

An operational guide for Regional Office (RO) and Schools Division Office (SDO) personnel on monitoring, evaluating, approving, and tracking the School Innovation and Improvement Fund (SIIF) across beneficiary schools.

A
SIIF Regional & Division Manual
Section Title
Follow the operational steps below.
A

Navigating InsightED

Access the official InsightED portal gateway and open the SIIF Monitoring Hub module.

Video · INSIGHTED Access

Step 1: Access the SIIF Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official portal link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/
  2. Click the ENTER INSIGHTED button.
  3. Select HROD Portals from the available portal cards.
  4. Click Continue as RO/SDO Personnel.
  5. Click the SIIF Monitoring Hub card to launch the module.
B

Login and Registration

Sign into your official RO/SDO account and complete account registration.

Video · Account Sign In & Registration

Step 1: Sign in with Official Account Credentials (Login)

  1. Enter your registered official DepEd Email address.
  2. Enter your authorized Password. If logging in via Passcode, toggle to the passcode option and input your designated quick sign-in code.
  3. Click the Sign In button to log into the platform.

Step 2: Create a New Account (Registration)

  1. Click the Create New Account button on the login screen.
  2. Fill out all required fields, including your First Name, Last Name, assigned Region / Division Scope, official Office Unit (SGOD, HRMO, or FTAD), and designated Role / Position (including Regional SBM Coordinator or Division SBM Coordinator).
  3. Submit your registration.
Mandatory Access Restriction: Access to the SIIF Monitoring Hub is strictly authorized for personnel assigned to the School Governance and Operations Division (SGOD), Human Resources and Management Office (HRMO), or Field Technical Assistance Division (FTAD), as well as personnel holding the official designation of Regional SBM Coordinator or Division SBM Coordinator. Accounts outside these authorized office and designations will receive an automatic Access Restricted security guard screen.

I

Division Home Tab

Monitor division progress metrics, filter schools by district, view visual accomplishment charts, and export summary reports.

Video · Division Dashboard

Step 1: Monitor Summary KPI Cards

  • Total Scope: View total beneficiary schools expected to submit plans in your division. (Hover to see breakdown per district).
  • Plan Accomplished: Track total submitted plans awaiting review or already reviewed.
  • Pending Submission: Identify schools that have not yet submitted their plan.
  • Financial Allocation: View total DepEd budget allocated for your division.

Step 2: Filter & Search Schools

  • Cascading Dropdowns: Select Division, District, or Municipality to filter records.
  • Status & Category: Filter by status (Submitted, Reviewed, Pending, Overdue) or school type.
  • Search Bar: Type a School Name or 6-digit School ID. Click Reset to clear.

Step 3: Review Progress Charts & Export

  • Accomplishment Gauge: Track review progress toward 100% completion.
  • Status Donut Chart: Click any color segment to view matching schools.
  • Export Report: Click Export Report to download a summary CSV spreadsheet.
II

Division Schools Tab

Inspect submitted proposals, check review schedule dates, evaluate compliance, and confirm reviews or request revisions.

Video · Plan Evaluation & Review Action

Step 1: Filter School Proposals

  • Click top status cards (Total Scope, Submitted, Reviewed, Pending, Overdue) to isolate school lists.
  • Toggle between Table View and Cards View.
Important System Rule — Official Review Window Schedule
Evaluations and review confirmations are enabled only during the active Review Window schedule shown on the system banner. Outside active dates, review action buttons are locked to enforce DepEd schedule policy.

Step 2: Inspect Detailed Proposal

  • Click any school row to open the slide-up School Detailed Proposal Sheet.
  • Verify requested budget estimates against the official allocation ceiling bar.
  • Review Priority Improvement Areas, Interventions , Beneficiaries, and Activities.
  • Click Printable Report Download if a hardcopy copy is needed.

Step 3: Evaluate & Action Proposals (Decision Paths)

  • Path A — Confirm Review (Approved): Click the green Confirm Review (Check) button, enter optional evaluation remarks, and confirm status as Reviewed.
  • Path B — Request Revision (Adjustments Needed): Click the Coordination Chat icon to message the School Head detailing required corrections. Re-inspect and confirm once resubmitted.

Step 4: Status Badge Lifecycle

Status Badge Meaning in Roster Required Action
Pending / Draft School Head is preparing or revising the proposal. Monitor progress; remind if deadline approaches.
Submitted Proposal submitted; ready for Division evaluation. Inspect proposal, send chat for revisions, or confirm review.
Reviewed Proposal evaluated and reviewed by Division Office. Baseline locked; school proceeds to quarterly tracking.
Overdue School missed expected submission deadline. Issue advisory via chat or dispatch technical assistance.

Pre-Review and Post-Review Operations Checklist

Use this operational checklist before opening the review window or generating final monitoring reports for regional leadership.

Step 2: Proposal Evaluation Quality Standards

  1. Logical Coherence: Priority improvement areas, interventions, beneficiaries, activities, and budget estimates form one unified, logical intervention.
  2. No Empty / Zero Items: Reject proposals containing interventions with zero beneficiaries, zero activities, or blank budget entries.
  3. ARAL Program Compliance: Verify subject-specific learner counts for remediation activities under ARAL guidelines.
  4. Strict Ceiling Compliance: Reject any plan where total estimated budget exceeds assigned school allocation.

Step 3: Track Post-Review & Quarterly Operations

  1. Verify that all submitted plans in the division have reached Reviewed status before the review deadline.
  2. Export the finalized Division/Region Masterlist report for administrative archiving via the Export Report button.
  3. Transition to Utilization Tracking across cover periods (JULY-SEPT, OCT-DEC, JAN-MARCH).
  4. Monitor quarterly spending reports and provide technical assistance to lagging schools.
Final Operating Rule: Maintain the complete SIIF cycle: Allocation verification ➔ Proposal submission oversight ➔ Rigorous SDO review & confirmation ➔ Quarterly utilization tracking ➔ Regional performance reporting.
